## Anti-slop baseline

professional-ready result; a stable production candidate should score at least 3 on every required
dimension.

## What is already strong

- The outputs are paper-specific rather than generic summaries. Debate separates an idealized
  complexity result, a premise about honest play, an MNIST proof of concept, and unresolved
  generalization. AssemblyHands reports concrete comparisons such as 21.92 versus 32.91 mm and
  surfaces confounds including the OpenPose missing-keypoint rate.
- Source anchoring is unusually strong. Exact passages, pages, token spans, and rectangles make the
  claims auditable and provide a sound base for a genuine reading path.
- Calibration is generally honest. Neither output treats a proposal, benchmark result, later use,
  and field-wide validation as interchangeable.

## Recurrent failure patterns

1. **Coverage substitutes for selection.** Fixed-looking quotation quotas and all-section reading
   paths dilute the promise that the interface has found the small portion worth reading.
2. **Glossaries are inventories, not ramps.** Terms are not tied to the passage that makes them
   necessary, and no dependency graph makes forward references measurable.
3. **Professional usefulness stops one step early.** The analyses explain what happened but rarely
   turn it into “inspect this result,” “check this assumption,” or “this observation would change
   the conclusion.”
4. **External context is synthesized but not decomposed.** Citation, operational reuse,
   independent corroboration, shorthand, and documented overclaiming need separate evidence.
5. **Opposition is often a caveat list.** A strong skeptical view should target one load-bearing
   inference, state the consequence if it fails, give the authors' best reply, and identify an
   observable adjudicator.

## Experimental corrections before interpreting wins

- Do not expose the experiment name or treatment-oriented research question to either generation
  arm; keep them as run metadata for the evaluator.
- Wire the catalog's reader baseline into generation rather than hardcoding a persona.
- Give each concurrent arm isolated schema and output paths.
- Score each arm absolutely with a factuality gate before collecting a relative preference. A
  relative vote alone cannot distinguish “both good” from “both slop.”
- For reception and camp experiments, retrieve one evidence dossier and give the identical dossier
  to both arms. Independent web searches confound prompting with different evidence.
- Explore the affected component with at least three generations per condition and paper; confirm
  a winner later in a complete learning-ramp output.

## Next hypotheses, in priority order

1. A source-anchored mechanism/evidence ladder will improve early traction and actionability more
   reliably than a richer general summary.
2. A minimum glossary whose entries declare dependencies and target passage IDs will outperform a
   flat “all important terms” glossary at equal term count.
3. A measured reading budget covering question → mechanism → evidence → qualification will create
   better triage than independent importance-ranked quotations.
4. One operational bridge applied to a real claim or equation, with an explicit failure point, will
   outperform bridges attached to every unfamiliar concept.
5. A single assumption stress test—if false, which inference changes, how to adjudicate it, and the
   best author reply—will be more useful than a general limitations section.
6. Fixed-corpus reception synthesis will be more reproducible and auditable than letting each arm
   conduct its own search.

The first completed A/B report should retain this baseline, add arm-level rubric scores and failure
tags, and state clearly when evidence is insufficient rather than promoting a pleasing example.
