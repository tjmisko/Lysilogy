## Functional measurements only: eight-paper fixture

`python3 scripts/bench/smoke.py` built a debug backend and production frontend, generated exactly
eight small PDFs, and drove the actual isolated app with Playwright Chromium 151.0.7922.34.
All eight real extraction artifacts were populated, three fresh browser contexts rendered the
library, and sixty deterministic title/author/year searches returned independently checked
results. The screenshot was inspected. No external requests, model calls, analysis, imports,
note mutations, or user-library access occurred. Temporary fixture PDFs/artifacts were removed;
derived observations are retained in
[synthetic-benchmark-fixture.json](../../eval/evidence/synthetic-benchmark-fixture.json).
