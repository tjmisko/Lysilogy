## How this record is checked

This task extracts what the paper itself says, and the application checks your work: every quote is compared against the page text above, and anything that is not verbatim is repaired or removed.

- Quotes are copied, never written. Every `quote` must be copied exactly from the page text, character for character, with the page it is on. Do not fix typos, expand abbreviations, or join sentences from different places. If you cannot find supporting text, leave `evidence` empty rather than inventing it.
- An evidence quote is 8 to 300 characters: one sentence or a clause is ideal.
- Do not invent references, numbers, authors or headings. An empty list is better than a guess, because a reader cannot tell an invented entry from a real one.
- Where a field asks for your judgment rather than the paper's words, give it, and say why.
- Where a `confidence` is asked for: `high` means the paper states it directly in the passage you quote; `medium` means the paper says it, but not in one place; `low` means you had to infer it or the text was hard to read.
